📐 How Cost/u is calculated
Avg feed cost = the coin cost of feeding this pet Easy + Medium + Hard once each (averaged across whichever foods you've checked in each list), converted to FLOWER. That's a full day's food bill.

For each resource: Cost/u = Avg feed cost ÷ (units you'd get if every bit of the day's energy went into fetching only that resource). It's a "what if" per resource, not a blended real-day cost — so Acorn/Fossil Shell (not tradable) always show as a full loss, since they get charged the whole day's food bill with no sale to offset it.
💰 How 24h Profit/Loss is calculated
For each resource: Gross = totalYield × marketplace sell price. Sell fee = Gross × your trade fee %. Net = Gross − Sell fee − the full day's Avg feed cost. The 🏆 Best resource is whichever tradable resource has the highest Net if you spent 100% of the day's energy on it alone.

Rates, fees & preferences
Coin ⇄ FLOWER Rate
1 FLOWER = Coins
Seeds and tools are bought with Coins, but items sell for FLOWER. This rate converts Coin cost into FLOWER so profit is calculated on the same currency.
Gem Purchase Reference
Spent FLOWER for Gems
Gems get cheaper the more you buy at once (bonding curve pricing), so there's no single fixed rate. Open the Buy Gems → FLOWER purchase screen, pick whatever bundle you actually buy, and enter the exact FLOWER cost and Gems amount shown there. The ratio below recalculates live — re-enter these anytime you buy a different (discounted) bundle to keep it accurate.
Gem Restock Strategy
Tools always cost 10 Gems to restock — that doesn't change no matter which button you pick. Seeds cost 15 Gems restocked alone, or effectively 10 Gems when bundled into a 20-Gem "All" restock (20 ÷ 2). Pick "All" only if you actually need both a tool and a seed restock at the same time — otherwise you're paying for a restock you didn't need. This choice feeds directly into every 24h profit calc.
Restock Cost Mode
Shared Cost Individual Cost
Shared Cost (default): splits the restock (and Shrine) cost evenly across every item checked below. Individual Cost: assumes one continuous use case, so the full restock/Shrine cost is charged to that single item — tap the ❓ above for details.

Restock Cost Mode

How Shared vs Individual cost affects profit/loss across the app.

Individual Cost — assumes you restock for a single, continuous use case, such as planting Barley for the entire day or week without switching to other crops. The full restock cost is charged to that one item alone. For example, a 15-Gem seed restock is shouldered entirely by Barley, with no portion allocated to any other crop.

Shared Cost (default) — assumes a single restock is used across multiple crops or tools, so its cost is divided evenly among everything included. For example, a 15-Gem seed restock is split across all seeds you've checked in the panel above, so each one only carries its proportional share instead of the full amount.

Uncheck an item in the panel above and hit 💾 Save to remove it from the shared pool — it reverts to a restock/Shrine cost of 0, and the remaining pool is redistributed among what's left.

This setting applies to every tab with a restock or Shrine cost — Crops, Fruits, Greenhouse, Animals, Resources and the Crop Machine — so switching modes updates the profit/loss figures shown across the entire app, not just in Settings.

Profit Mode
24hrs (default) shows profit/loss as a daily rate — this is what every calculation in the app is based on and nothing changes when you switch. Per Stock only changes what's displayed on each card: instead of a daily rate, it shows the total cost, yield, and profit for using up everything currently in stock for that item (e.g. all 200 Axes you have banked) — a snapshot, not a new calculation mode.
